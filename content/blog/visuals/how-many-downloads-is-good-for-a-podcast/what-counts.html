What the number on your dashboard means

A download and a Spotify play are two different counts

Your host

Download

  • Counts when enough of the file to play 1 minute has downloaded
  • Repeat requests from the same IP and app are filtered, typically over a 24-hour window
  • Bots and data center traffic are filtered out
  • Covers every app that pulls your feed. It does not prove a listen
Spotify

Play

  • Counts when an episode is watched or listened to for at least 30 seconds
  • Only counts listening on Spotify
  • Shown in Spotify for Creators analytics
  • Will not match your host's download count
