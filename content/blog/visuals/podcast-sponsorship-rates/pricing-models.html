Podcast sponsorship pricing models

Four ways to price a sponsor, and who carries the risk

CPM

Per 1,000 downloads

Risk: shared. Price follows the downloads.

Fits: shows where downloads × CPM gives an invoice worth sending.

Flat rate

Per episode or season

Risk: on the sponsor. The price holds whatever the downloads.

Fits: niche shows that sell access to a clear audience, with extras priced as lines.

Affiliate

Cost per acquisition

Risk: on the host. No sales, no pay.

Fits: any size. Buzzsprout says there is no download minimum.

Revenue share

Platform sells the ads

Risk: shared. You keep a set share, such as Spotify's 50%.

Fits: shows that meet the platform's bar and do not want to sell.

Sources: Buzzsprout sponsor guide (13 Jan 2026), Spotify Partner Program help page, read 26 Nov 2026.SLK Media Agency
