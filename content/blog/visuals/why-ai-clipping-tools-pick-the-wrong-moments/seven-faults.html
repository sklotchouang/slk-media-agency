Fault and fix

Seven faults in AI clips, and the fix for each

The wrong moment was picked
1

The moment needs setupA strong middle loses to a weaker clip that stands alone

FixAdd the setup line back, or one line of on-screen context
2

Detail beats storyA detailed explanation was flagged, a short story was missed

FixNote your own timestamps, then search the transcript for them
3

The length setting cut the storyA 90-second story does not fit a 30-second target

FixRaise the length setting, or cut the story by hand
4

The best moments sit close togetherChoppity avoids many clips from one 60-second stretch

FixCheck the minutes on either side of every good clip
5

Crosstalk muddied the transcriptOverlapping voices lower transcript confidence

FixRecord or upload a separate track per speaker
A good moment was cut badly
6

The clip starts mid-sentenceA timestamp two seconds off loses the hook

FixDrag the cut point, then replay the first three seconds
7

The wrong speaker is in frameTracking jumps when two people talk over each other

FixWatch every two-person clip and set the framing by hand
