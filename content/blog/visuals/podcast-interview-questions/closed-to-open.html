Closed question, open question

Same topic, a different answer

Closed
"Was that difficult?"
Gets a yes or a no. The guest waits for the next question.
Open
"What made that experience difficult for you?"
Asks for the reason, so the guest has to tell what happened.
Closed
"Did the launch go well?"
The guest says "it went fine" and the moment is gone.
Open
"Walk me through launch week, day by day."
Asks for a sequence, which brings out a story with a start and an end.
Double
"Why did you leave, and what did you learn?"
Two questions at once. The guest has to choose which part to answer.
One at a time
"What was the last straw before you left?"
One question, one clear answer. Ask the second one next.
