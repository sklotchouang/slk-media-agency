One interview, five stages

Order the questions from easy to hard, then land it

Stage 1

Open

One easy question about their work today. Not their childhood, and not their life story.

›
Stage 2

Story

A moment that changed things. Ask for the scene: where they were, what they did.

›
Stage 3

Expertise

The method, step by step. Ask for a real case, not a theory.

›
Stage 4

Tension

A mistake, a disagreement, an unpopular view. Rapport is built by now.

›
Stage 5

Close

One idea for the listener, then where to find the guest.

Our working order for a guest interview. The rule against opening on personal life is from Riverside's guide (updated 23 Jul 2026).SLK Media Agency
