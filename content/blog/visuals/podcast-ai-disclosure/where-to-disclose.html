Where a podcast AI disclosure goes

One sentence, three places.

1

In the audio

A spoken line near the start of the episode. Apple asks for it "in the content". Acast asks for it in the podcast file for a material portion.

AppleAcastEU: first exposure
2

In every description

One line near the top of each episode description, and one in the show description. Apple says "for each episode and show".

AppleAcast
3

At upload on YouTube

Use the altered or synthetic content setting when the AI part looks or sounds real. YouTube adds the label.

YouTube
Example line, written for this article (no platform requires set wording): "The ad read in this episode uses a synthetic voice made with AI. The conversation is real and unedited."
Sources: Apple rule 1.11, Acast help (3 Jun 2026), YouTube Help, EU AI Act Art. 50(5), read 26 Nov 2026.SLK Media Agency
