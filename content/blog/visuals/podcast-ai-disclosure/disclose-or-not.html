Podcast AI disclosure: disclose or not

Two questions sort every AI use.

1. Did AI make audio or video your audience hears or sees?A voice, a host, an ad read, a translation, footage, music
No: it helped with text or cleanup
No platform label neededScript, outline, interview questions, show notes, titles, captions, noise removal, silence cuts. YouTube and Acast name these as exempt. Apple's rule covers audio and video only.
↓
One EU exceptionAI text published to inform the public on matters of public interest, with no human review and no one holding editorial responsibility.
Yes: AI made sound or pictures
2. Does it copy a real person's voice or face?
YesGet written permission. Disclose on Apple, Acast and YouTube, and under the EU AI Act. Spotify removes it without permission.
No, it is a made-up voiceApple: disclose in every episode and the show. Acast: if material or realistic. YouTube: if realistic.
Sources: Apple rules 1.11 and 1.12, Acast help, YouTube Help, Spotify newsroom, EU AI Act Art. 3 and 50, read 26 Nov 2026. Not legal advice.SLK Media Agency
