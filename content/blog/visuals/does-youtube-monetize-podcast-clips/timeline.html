YouTube monetization update, 15 July 2025

What changed for podcast clips, and what did not

Before 15 July 2025

Policy
"Repetitious content"

The old name of the rule on repetitive videos.

Policy
Reused content

Covers "commentary, clips, compilations, and reaction videos".

➜➜

After 15 July 2025

Renamed
"Inauthentic content"

Now says it covers content that is "repetitive or mass-produced", including template-made AI videos.

No change
Reused content

The rule your podcast clips live under. YouTube: "There is no change to our reused content policy".
