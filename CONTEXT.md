# SLK Media Agency pricing

The packages SLK Media Agency sells to podcasters, and the words every page, agent and price card uses for them.

## Language

**Starter service**:
The lighter done-for-you service: a dedicated project manager, a strategy document before the first clip, thumbnails, captions and posting, with one call and one report a month. Sold as Done For You Starter and on the calculator below 20 clips.
_Avoid_: Lite, Light, Done For You Lite

**Done For You service**:
The full done-for-you service: everything in the Starter service, with a weekly call and a weekly report instead of monthly ones. Sold from 20 clips upward.
_Avoid_: Full service (on its own), Content Engine (that is the card label, not the service)

**Done For You Starter**:
The package that delivers the Starter service on 10 clips a month. Card label "Content Starter".
_Avoid_: Starter plan at 12 clips (the scope before 27 October 2026)

**Done With You**:
Clips edited and handed over; the client posts them. No project manager, calls, reports or posting.
_Avoid_: Clips Only

**Promotion price**:
The price shown on /pricing/promotion to the first 4 new creators, kept for as long as they stay.
_Avoid_: Discount, sale price

**Normal price**:
The price on /pricing, shown struck through on the promotion page.
