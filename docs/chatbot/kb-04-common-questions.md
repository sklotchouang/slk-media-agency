# SLK Media Agency: common questions and objections

## Do you guarantee download growth or sponsor revenue?

No, and you should run from anyone who does. SLK Media Agency controls content production and distribution, not your recording quality, your guest selection, how aggressively you pursue sponsor outreach, or market conditions in your niche. What SLK Media Agency does commit to is the clip volume your plan states delivered on time, multi platform distribution on schedule, and weekly performance reports.

## What is the difference between the two monthly plans?

Clip volume, and nothing else. Content Engine Lite delivers 10 clips a month, Content Engine delivers 20. Everything else is identical.

## Is the $597 trial charged again?

No. It is a single payment. One time, no subscription, nothing to cancel. Note that the $597 excludes the processing fee, which is added at checkout and brings the total to $617.

## Am I locked into a contract?

No. The monthly plans are month to month. Cancel any time before your next billing cycle, with zero penalties. You can pause for up to 60 days instead. The notice period is 14 days. You own everything SLK Media Agency ever made for you.

SLK Media Agency does not trap clients in contracts, because it does not need to. Clients stay because the work keeps earning its place.

## Is it safe to hand over my episode files?

SLK Media Agency signs NDAs for all clients. Your content, guest information and strategic plans are confidential. You retain 100% ownership of all content created for you.

## Why not just use an AI clipping tool like Descript or OpusClip?

AI tools are for podcasters testing the waters with content repurposing. This is for podcasters serious about scaling revenue. AI tools optimize for engagement metrics and clip the moments an algorithm thinks are interesting, usually the loud ones. Nobody writes your captions, nobody builds a strategy, nobody posts it, and nobody tells you what worked. SLK Media Agency clips for sponsor appeal, then writes it, thumbnails it, schedules it, publishes it, and reports on it. AI tools save you time. SLK Media Agency takes the whole job off your desk.

Using an auto clipping tool is not a reason to walk away from a conversation. It is the clearest place to show the difference.

## I already use an auto clipping tool.

Those tools cut clips, but they do not pick the moments that will actually perform, they do not format per platform, and they do not sustain a posting cadence. That selection and consistency layer is what SLK Media Agency does.

## Why not just hire a freelancer for $200 a month?

Because you are not buying editing hours, you are buying a finished content operation. A freelancer is $200 a month plus roughly 4 hours a week managing them, which is about $1,800 a month all in, for 5 to 8 clips and no strategy. A freelancer costs you management time on top of their fee, and still leaves you as the bottleneck.

## Why not hire an in house editor?

A full time in house video editor in the US runs $50,000 to $80,000 a year, before software, equipment and management time.

## I can hire someone cheaper.

You definitely can, there is no shortage of editors. The difference is that SLK Media Agency is not selling editing, it is selling growth: which moments will perform, which platforms to prioritize, and how to build momentum that attracts sponsors. That is not what you get from a freelancer cutting clips.

## So you just make clips?

Not quite. Freelancers and VAs edit clips, which is why they are easy to replace. SLK Media Agency helps shows grow and attract sponsors by finding the segments that will actually perform and distributing them where your audience scrolls most. The clips are the vehicle. Growth is what gets delivered.

## I already have an agency or editor handling this.

If a human team is picking the clips and posting them and it is working, that is a solid setup and SLK Media Agency will leave you to it.

## I am not sure it would pay off.

That is a fair thing to want to check. The published case studies and the seven video testimonials are the honest evidence, and Content Engine Lite at $997 a month is the lower commitment monthly path. There is no projected number to offer, because SLK Media Agency does not publish averages.

## Not right now, maybe later.

That is completely fine. Timing matters more than anything else here.

## What if none of these fit my budget right now?

Then this is not the right time, and SLK Media Agency will say so rather than sell you something that will not work. The monthly plans start at $997 a month. If a monthly content budget is not realistic for your show yet, start with Descript (about $30 a month) or OpusClip (about $50 a month). Come back when the timing is right.

## Do you do the sponsor outreach for me?

No. SLK Media Agency does not cold email brands, negotiate rates, or close deals. What it does is build clips designed to demonstrate sponsor friendly messaging, weekly performance reports you can show to brands, and sponsor signal tracking. SLK Media Agency builds the visibility. You close the partnerships.

## Can you work with my niche?

Almost certainly. SLK Media Agency has produced content for B2B SaaS and sales podcasts, health, wellness and nutrition experts, financial planning and investing shows, real estate and entrepreneurship podcasts, comedy, storytelling and true crime shows, and technical deep dives in AI, blockchain and biotech. An unfamiliar niche gets researched until the team are experts, and that is included in onboarding.

## Am I a good fit?

A good fit is publishing 2 or more episodes a month, having been going for 6 or more months with at least 15 episodes live, being ready to invest in professional content production, and wanting to turn the podcast into a revenue channel.

A poor fit is a show with fewer than 15 episodes published, podcasting as a hobby or an experimental side project, or someone just exploring options.
