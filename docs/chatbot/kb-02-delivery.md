# SLK Media Agency: how the work actually gets done

You send your episodes. SLK Media Agency does the rest on the monthly plans.

## The process

The monthly process runs in eight steps: you submit raw episodes and brand assets, then onboarding and strategy, then human editorial review to find the high retention moments, then production (short form edit, captions, pacing, hooks), then platform adaptation, then quality control, then scheduling and distribution, then an ongoing optimization loop.

On the 10-Day Trial the process stops after quality control. SLK Media Agency edits the 5 clips and hands them over, and you do the scheduling and posting yourself.

Your first clips arrive within 3 to 5 business days after onboarding.

## Onboarding

Days 1 to 4: the team immerses itself in your niche, covering competitor analysis, sponsor research and audience psychology.

Days 5 to 8: a test batch of clips is produced and iterated on your feedback.

From day 9: the work runs autonomously with weekly strategy syncs.

## Platforms and formats

Platforms covered: Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, Facebook and YouTube Shorts. You choose the focus.

Captions and hashtags are customized per platform on the monthly plans.

## What you provide

You provide full episodes, audio or video, plus your branding. On the monthly plans SLK Media Agency handles everything after that, including the copywriting and the posting.

You review and approve clips before they go live on the monthly plans.

The service takes less than 1 hour a week of your time.

## Reporting

Success is tracked through views, engagement, follower growth and click through rate, reported weekly on the monthly plans. The weekly reports cover which clips got the most views and engagement, which drove sponsor adjacent traffic, a platform comparison, sponsor signals, and recommendations for the next week.

## Publishing frequency

SLK Media Agency works best with shows publishing 2 to 4 episodes a month. Content volume drives sponsor visibility, and one episode a month leaves gaps in the posting calendar.

If you publish one episode a month, SLK Media Agency is happy to strategize on increasing output sustainably. Many clients have shifted from 1 episode a month to 2 or 3 with better planning.

The service works for new podcasters.

## Niches

SLK Media Agency has produced content for B2B SaaS and sales podcasts, health, wellness and nutrition experts, financial planning and investing shows, real estate and entrepreneurship podcasts, comedy, storytelling and true crime shows, and technical deep dives in AI, blockchain and biotech.

If your podcast is in a niche SLK Media Agency has not tackled, the team researches it until they are experts, and that research is included in onboarding.

## Confidentiality and ownership

SLK Media Agency signs NDAs for all clients. Your content, guest information and strategic plans are confidential. SLK Media Agency has worked with corporate podcasts discussing unreleased product strategies, health professionals navigating HIPAA compliance, and financial advisors with client confidentiality requirements.

SLK Media Agency never shares your information or project details without permission.

You retain 100% ownership of all content SLK Media Agency creates. If you leave, you keep everything forever.

## Sponsor outreach

SLK Media Agency does not do sponsor outreach. It does not cold email brands, negotiate rates, or close deals.

What it does is build clips designed to demonstrate sponsor friendly messaging, weekly performance reports you can show to brands, and sponsor signal tracking. SLK Media Agency builds the visibility. You close the partnerships.
