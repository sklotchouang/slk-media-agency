# Starter gets a monthly call and report, and owns every volume below 20 clips

Samuel, 27 October 2026 (growth item 19). Done For You Starter changes from 12 clips with the weekly Done For You service to 10 clips at $697 a month (normal $997) with a monthly call and a monthly report, to free project manager hours. The dedicated PM, the strategy document, thumbnails, captions and posting stay. Because the old calculator priced Done For You at 10 clips at $622 with weekly calls, a $697 Starter would have been undercut by a richer service, so the calculator now sells the Starter service below 20 clips ($697 at 10, plus $30 per clip from 11 to 19, minus $37.50 per clip below 10, 6 clips = $547), and Done For You ($997) and Done For You Plus ($1,597) start at 20 clips.

## Considered options

- Done For You only from 20 clips, Starter a fixed card with $50 extra clips: rejected, it removes custom volumes between 11 and 19.
- Leaving the calculator as it was: rejected, it sells weekly service for less than Starter.
- Keeping 12 clips, or lowering the price to $597: Samuel chose 10 clips at $697.

## Consequences

- No client was on Starter on 27 October 2026 (production database checked), so no live service changed. Older plans (Javier Boix, Corvette Today) are untouched.
- Every surface that maps a price to a scope must now say "monthly" for anything under 20 clips and "weekly" from 20 clips.
